## Products

| Product | Question | Template | Tenor | Listed | Pays Yes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SHCU Monthly Touch** | Will SHCU reach this level at any time before expiry? | `priceTouch` | Monthly | 4 targets | On first touch. Settles early |
| **SHCU Quarterly Close** | Will SHCU close above this level on expiry day? | `binaryPrice` | Quarterly | 3 strikes | At expiry only |

Seven outcomes are live at any time, and 60 are listed per year: 48 monthly targets and 12 quarterly strikes.

<Info>
  Both products resolve on the SHCU oracle, the same reference as the `io:SHCU` perpetual. A hedge in the perpetual carries no basis to the settlement price.
</Info>

## How an outcome share works

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Tokens | Each outcome has a Yes token and a No token |
| Price | Between 0 and 1 USDC. The Yes price reads as the market's probability |
| Collateral | Fully collateralized. One Yes plus one No is always worth exactly 1 USDC |
| Leverage and liquidation | None. The most a buyer can lose is the premium paid |
| Order book | Yes and No books are merged. Buying Yes at `p` is the same order as selling No at `1 − p` |
| Settlement | Yes converts to 1 USDC and No to 0 if the outcome resolves Yes, and the reverse if it resolves No |
| Settlement currency | USDC |
| Size | Whole shares |

## Design basis: SHFE copper options

Strikes and expiries follow SHFE copper futures options, so the grid is the one copper desks already use. Collateral and settlement follow HIP-4.

| Parameter | SHFE copper option | SHCU outcome markets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Underlying | SHFE copper futures, 5 tonnes | SHCU oracle |
| Price | Premium in CNY/t | Probability, 0 to 1 USDC |
| Strike grid | 500, 1,000 or 2,000 CNY/t by price level | Same grid. 2,000 CNY/t above 80,000 |
| Strikes listed | Settlement ± 1.5× daily limit | Touch: ATM ± 2 and ± 4 intervals. Close: ATM and ATM ± 4 intervals |
| Style | American | Touch pays on first touch. Close resolves at expiry |
| Expiry | 5th-last trading day of the month before the futures delivery month | The same day, at 07:00 UTC (15:00 Beijing) |
| Settlement | Exercise into a futures position | Cash, in USDC |
| Margin | Seller posts margin | Fully collateralized |

**ATM** is the SHCU reference price at listing, rounded to the nearest strike interval.

## SHCU Monthly Touch

| Term | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Market | One per month, named by expiry month: *SHCU Monthly Touch Nov 2026* |
| Outcomes | 4 `priceTouch` targets: 2 above and 2 below ATM |
| Targets | ATM ± 2 and ATM ± 4 strike intervals. With a 2,000 CNY/t interval: ± 4,000 and ± 8,000 CNY/t |
| Listing | On the prior month's expiry |
| Touch condition | The SHCU oracle is at or beyond the target at any time before the resolution time |
| Resolution time | 07:00 UTC on the SHFE copper option expiry day |
| Settlement, Yes | Settled to 1 within 1 hour of the touch |
| Settlement, No | Settled to 0 at expiry |
| Disruption | Per the `priceTouch` template |

**The touch is monitored on the oracle in every session state.** During Shanghai hours that is the index. Outside them it is the bounded-discovery oracle, which follows the SHCU book with a 30-minute time constant inside a ±1,000 bps band. A touch outside Shanghai hours therefore requires the SHCU book's impact mid to trade beyond the target for long enough to carry the oracle there. At a reopen the oracle steps to the first fresh index print, and a step through the target is a touch. See [Oracle](/oracle).

## SHCU Quarterly Close

| Term | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Market | One per quarter, named by expiry month: *SHCU Quarterly Close Dec 2026* |
| Outcomes | 3 `binaryPrice` strikes |
| Strikes | ATM and ATM ± 4 strike intervals. With a 2,000 CNY/t interval: ATM and ± 8,000 CNY/t |
| Listing | On the prior quarter's expiry |
| Expiry | SHFE copper option expiry day in March, June, September and December |
| Resolution price | 300-second TWAP of the SHCU oracle ending at the resolution time |
| Resolution time | 07:00 UTC (15:00 Beijing) on expiry day |
| Resolution | Yes if the resolution price is above the strike. Otherwise No |
| Settlement | Settled to 1 or 0 within 1 hour of the resolution time |
| Disruption | If the oracle is stale for the full window: the same window on the prior Shanghai trading day |

**The Close resolves on index prints.** The TWAP window is 14:55 to 15:00 Beijing time, the last five minutes of the Shanghai afternoon session. In that window the oracle is the index in passthrough. Off-hours discovery plays no part in a Quarterly Close resolution unless the disruption rule applies. A resolution price exactly equal to the strike resolves No.

## Pricing and hedging

**One reference.** The outcomes and the perpetual read the same oracle. A market maker who hedges an outcome in `io:SHCU` carries no basis between the hedge instrument and the settlement price. The residual risks are the ones inherent to the payoff: gamma near the strike into expiry, and gap risk across closures.

**Close.** A Quarterly Close Yes share is a cash-or-nothing digital call on the five-minute TWAP. Its price is the risk-neutral probability that the TWAP finishes above the strike. The three strikes together mark three points on the market-implied distribution of Shanghai copper at quarter end.

**Touch.** A Monthly Touch Yes share is a one-touch barrier on the oracle path. For a driftless underlying, the probability of touching a level before expiry is close to twice the probability of finishing beyond it, which gives a first check between Touch prices and digital prices at the same level.

**Strikes are in CNY/t and the payout is a fixed 1 USDC.** There is no quanto adjustment on the payout. The quanto treatment applies only to the perpetual used as the hedge. See [SHCU contract specification](/contract-specification).

| Opening a position or minting a pair | None |

| Closing as maker | 0.060% of notional |
| Settlement | 0.105% of the payout, so it falls on the winning side |

These are Hyperliquid's outcome rates multiplied by 1.5, which is what a `deployerFeeScale` of 0.5 produces. Volume tiers and staking discounts apply. See [Fees](/fees).

## Settlement integrity

Outcomes are settled onchain by the deployer strictly according to the template parameters above. HIP-4 has no trader dispute window. A HIP-4 deployer's 500,000 HYPE stake can be slashed by validator vote for a poorly defined market, a settlement that does not follow its template, or a market left unsettled for more than one week.
