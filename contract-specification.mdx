## Reading the quote

<Info>
  The price on screen is the Shanghai price. `110,383` means 110,383 CNY per tonne. It is not a USD price and it is not rescaled.
</Info>

One contract references one tonne. Position value and PnL are computed on the CNY number and paid in USDC, one for one:

```text
Notional (USDC) = |size| × price
PnL (USDC)      = size × (exit price − entry price)
```

| Example | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Long 5 contracts at 110,000 | Notional 550,000 USDC. Initial margin at 10×: 55,000 USDC |
| Price rises to 111,500 | PnL = 5 × 1,500 = +7,500 USDC, a 1.36% return on notional |
| Minimum increment, 0.00001 contract at 110,383 | 1.10 USDC of notional |
| One tick, 1 CNY/t, on one contract | 1 USDC |

## What "quanto" means here

The index is a CNY price. The contract pays in USDC. No exchange rate connects the two: the CNY price move is paid as the same number of USDC. Three consequences matter.

**Percentage returns are Shanghai copper's CNY returns.** A 1% move in the index is a 1% gain or loss on USDC notional. USD/CNY does not enter the PnL.

**A contract carries more copper delta than one tonne.** One contract gains 1 USDC when Shanghai copper rises 1 CNY/t. A physical tonne gains 1 CNY, which is 1/S dollars at a USD/CNY rate of S. One contract therefore has the dollar delta of S tonnes: about 7 tonnes at USD/CNY 7.00. Its USDC notional equals the dollar value of that quantity of copper.

**The hedge ratio is a division by the exchange rate.** To hedge T tonnes of CNY-priced copper:

```text
contracts = T ÷ S        where S is USD/CNY
```

| Exposure | USD/CNY | Contracts | Notional at 110,000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 140 tonnes | 7.00 | 20 | 2,200,000 USDC |
| 1,000 tonnes | 7.00 | 142.86 | 15,714,286 USDC |

If the index falls 2,000 CNY/t, 140 tonnes of inventory loses 280,000 CNY, which is 40,000 dollars at 7.00. A short of 20 contracts gains 20 × 2,000 = 40,000 USDC.

The ratio drifts as USD/CNY moves, so a hedge sized today is re-sized as the rate changes. Hedging SHCU itself against SHFE, LME or COMEX is covered in [Index behavior and hedging](/index-behavior-and-hedging).

## Order and price conventions

| Convention | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Price | Integer CNY per tonne at current levels. Hyperliquid accepts up to 5 significant figures, and integer prices are always valid |
| Size | Contracts (tonnes), to 5 decimal places |
| Order types | All Hyperliquid order types: limit, market, stop, take-profit, TWAP, scale, with post-only, IOC and GTC time-in-force |
| Notional for caps, margin tiers and depth measures | \|size\| × price, in USDC |

## Funding

Funding is exchanged between longs and shorts every hour and is computed on oracle-valued notional. The premium is the distance of the book's impact mid from the oracle. While it averages between 2 bps below and 4 bps above the oracle, the rate is 0.005% per 8 hours, paid by longs, which is about 5.5% a year. See [Funding](/funding).

## Fees

At the base Hyperliquid tier the all-in fee is 0.9 bp for takers and 0.3 bp for makers. See [Fees](/fees).

## Open interest

The cap is $100,000,000 of notional, about 906 contracts at 110,383. It rises in five stages to $1,000,000,000 as published gates are met. See [Open interest caps](/open-interest-caps).
