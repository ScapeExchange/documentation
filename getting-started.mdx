## Trade the perpetual

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a Hyperliquid account holding USDC">
    There is no Scape account to open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open io:SHCU">
    Find it in a Hyperliquid interface that lists builder-deployed markets. It is on the `io` dex.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the io dex with USDC">
    Each builder-deployed dex is margined separately from the main perpetual account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose leverage">
    Up to 10×. Margin is isolated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the order">
    Enter a price in CNY per tonne and a size in contracts of one tonne.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the ticket before the first order

| On the ticket | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Price `110,383` | 110,383 CNY per tonne. It is not a dollar price |
| Size `5` | 5 contracts, referencing 5 tonnes |
| Order value | 5 × 110,383 = 551,915 USDC. Size × price, read as USDC, with no exchange rate |
| Margin at 10× | 55,191.50 USDC |
| One tick | 1 CNY/t, worth 1 USDC per contract |
| Smallest size | 0.00001 contract, about 1.10 USDC of notional. The minimum order value is 10 USDC |

## Check three things before holding a position

| Check | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session state | When Shanghai is closed, the oracle follows the SHCU book inside a band, and liquidity can be thinner | [Oracle](/oracle), [Trading calendar](/trading-calendar) |
| Liquidation price | A 10× long is liquidated 5.26% below entry on the mark price, at any hour | [Margin and liquidation](/margin-and-liquidation) |
| Funding | Paid or received every hour on the position's notional | [Funding](/funding) |

<Warning>
  A leveraged position can be liquidated at any hour, including while Shanghai is closed. Liquidation uses the mark price, which follows the SHCU book.
</Warning>

## Trade the outcome markets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use a Hyperliquid account holding USDC">
    The same account as for the perpetual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an outcome">
    Choose an SHCU Monthly Touch or SHCU Quarterly Close outcome in an interface that lists Hyperliquid outcome markets, or in an interface integrated with Skew.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy Yes or No">
    The price is between 0 and 1 USDC a share. Size is in whole shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold or close">
    A winning share settles to 1 USDC and a losing share to 0. A position can be sold on the book at any time before resolution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

There is no leverage and no liquidation on an outcome position. The most a buyer can lose is the price paid. See [Outcome markets](/outcome-markets).
